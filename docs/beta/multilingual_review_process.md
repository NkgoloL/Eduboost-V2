# Multilingual Content Review Process

**Status:** pending human educator review

Each non-English lesson sample must be reviewed by a qualified educator or fluent subject-matter reviewer for grammar, mathematical correctness, CAPS alignment, cultural appropriateness, and age-appropriate language.
