# PRD-7.5-7.9 Observability/SRE Final Assurance and Handoff

This record closes the PRD-7 observability, SRE, and incident-readiness stream.
It consolidates final evidence for dashboards, alerts, SLOs, incident runbooks,
on-call ownership, backup/restore, rollback, privacy escalation, telemetry PII
redaction, and support/status communications.

## Scope

- PRD-7.5 final observability/SRE evidence acceptance.
- PRD-7.6 incident-readiness reconciliation.
- PRD-7.7 SRE signoff and authority reconciliation.
- PRD-7.8 final PRD-7 evidence capture.
- PRD-7.9 controlled handoff to PRD-8.

## Boundaries

This slice does not authorise PRD-8 implementation, production release,
deployment, release tags, public beta, live learner traffic, billing launch, or
live payment processing. It records readiness and handoff only.
