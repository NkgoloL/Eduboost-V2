# KG-3 Learner Graph Shadow Policy

KG-3 may construct learner shadow states only as non-authoritative evidence artifacts.

Rules:

- Shadow states must be derived from the KG-2 target graph.
- Shadow observations must be synthetic or explicitly anonymised in a future approved slice.
- KG-3 must not read production learner records.
- KG-3 must not persist learner graph tables.
- KG-3 must not alter diagnostic, tutor, parent, study-plan, or gamification behaviour.
- KG-3 must not become the authority for learner-facing recommendations.
