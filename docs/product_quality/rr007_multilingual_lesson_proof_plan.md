# RR-007 Multilingual Lesson Proof Plan

Multilingual lesson proof planned: true

## Scope

A multilingual proof must demonstrate that generated or retrieved lesson content can preserve:

- CAPS grounding;
- mathematical meaning;
- grade-appropriate wording;
- safety and consent boundaries;
- source/provenance traceability.

## Boundary

This is a proof plan. It does not authorise broad multilingual content rollout.
