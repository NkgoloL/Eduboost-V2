# Cross-Cohort Replication Protocol (LEV-WS12)

## 1. Objective
Establishes a frozen analytical protocol to test whether psychometric calibration, false-mastery bounds, and impact effect sizes replicate across independent academic cohorts and calendar terms.

## 2. Replication Success Criteria
1. **Calibration Stability**: ECE across new cohort $\le 0.15$; Brier score within $\pm 0.05$ of baseline.
2. **False-Mastery Risk Bound**: FMR does not exceed baseline upper 95% Wilson confidence interval.
3. **Treatment Effect Replication**: Post-test gain $\beta_{\text{treat}} > 0$ with $p < 0.05$ and effect size within $80\%$ of baseline Hedges' $g$.
4. **Fairness Invariance**: Quintile demographic parity difference remains $\le 0.15$.
