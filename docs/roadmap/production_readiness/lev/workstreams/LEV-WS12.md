# LEV-WS12 — Replicate findings in another cohort or academic year

**Definition of done:** Findings replicate in another cohort or academic year.

**Objective:** Show that measurement and impact findings are not artefacts of one cohort, calendar period, school set, item form or model snapshot.

**Dependencies:** Completed initial longitudinal cohort; frozen replication protocol; new cohort access.

**Accountability:** Independent Research Lead (A); Psychometric Lead, Data Science Lead, School Operations Lead (R).

**Indicative duration:** 6-12 months after primary study

## Task execution order

1. [LEV-12.01](../tasks/LEV-12.01.md) — Identify the primary claims requiring replication and rank them by decision importance.
1. [LEV-12.02](../tasks/LEV-12.02.md) — Freeze the original protocol, analysis code, model, graph, item and outcome definitions.
1. [LEV-12.03](../tasks/LEV-12.03.md) — Pre-specify what will remain identical and what will intentionally vary in the replication.
1. [LEV-12.04](../tasks/LEV-12.04.md) — Select a later cohort or academic year and, where possible, additional schools or contexts.
1. [LEV-12.05](../tasks/LEV-12.05.md) — Refresh only items required for exposure control while preserving scale linkage through anchors.
1. [LEV-12.06](../tasks/LEV-12.06.md) — Prospectively capture predictions using the authorised model or explicitly versioned successor.
1. [LEV-12.07](../tasks/LEV-12.07.md) — Repeat calibration, classification, retention, transfer and state-reasonableness analyses.
1. [LEV-12.08](../tasks/LEV-12.08.md) — Repeat the impact analysis or a confirmatory effectiveness design where feasible.
1. [LEV-12.09](../tasks/LEV-12.09.md) — Compare effect direction, magnitude, uncertainty, calibration and subgroup patterns with the original.
1. [LEV-12.10](../tasks/LEV-12.10.md) — Investigate non-replication through population, curriculum, item, teacher-use, platform and model-change analyses.
1. [LEV-12.11](../tasks/LEV-12.11.md) — Run sensitivity analyses using the original and updated model versions on comparable data.
1. [LEV-12.12](../tasks/LEV-12.12.md) — Define which claims generalise, which require narrower scope and which must be withdrawn.
1. [LEV-12.13](../tasks/LEV-12.13.md) — Obtain independent review of replication strength and residual uncertainty.
1. [LEV-12.14](../tasks/LEV-12.14.md) — Set future replication cadence for major model families and high-impact uses.

## Closure rule

The workstream may close only when every task is closed or independently waived and the following condition is supported: **Findings replicate in another cohort or academic year.**
