# RR-018 Trustworthy Beta Product Quality

## Register citation

- RR item: `RR-018`
- Register title: `Trustworthy beta`
- Source: `docs/product/roadmap.md`
- Priority: `P1`

## Purpose

RR-018 closes the product-facing trustworthy-beta quality item: feedback/report issue button, content correction workflow, human review queue, and educator review of priority CAPS topics.

This slice is intentionally product-facing. It adds a frontend quality panel and service contracts, then requires final validation files before evidence capture.

## Boundary

RR-018 does not authorise production release, deployment, release tagging, public beta activation, public beta live traffic, billing launch, live payment processing, or Runtime KG implementation.
